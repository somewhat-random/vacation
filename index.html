Snorkel and dive

Ramon’s has its own dive shop on the dock, with boats out to the reef at 9:00, 11:30 and 2:00. Most reef sites are a 5 to 15 minute ride.

Already included: Hol Chan & Shark Ray Alley

A half-day snorkel for all four of us. Hol Chan is a marine reserve full of fish and turtles. At Shark Ray Alley, nurse sharks and stingrays swim right up to you.

Extra trips to vote on

Star anything you’d want to do. No limit here. The ride rating shows how much boat time each one has. Prices are what Ramon’s website lists and should be checked before booking.

Depth limits. Under PADI rules, junior divers ages 12 to 14 can go to 70 ft, so we’re sticking to dives that stay at or above that.

Diving before flying. Divers should wait at least 18 hours after multiple dives before flying. With a 10 AM flight on Dec 1, the last dive should be Monday morning.

Golf cart adventures

Golf carts are how everyone gets around Ambergris Caye. San Pedro town is in the middle, a bridge leads north, and a dirt road runs south. Star the places you want to go.

In town and south

Things to remember

  • Sneakers or hiking boots with good tread. Caves Branch requires them, and Tevas or Keens aren’t allowed.
  • An extra pair of sneakers to switch out, since things stay wet.
  • Lightweight long pants and shirts for the cave and jungle tours.
  • A flashlight or headlamp for each person.
  • Thick socks if we do ATM. The upper cave is socks only.
  • Passports with 6+ months left. We also need them for Tikal.
  • Dive certification cards and logbooks.
  • DEET bug spray, reef-safe sunscreen, swimsuits.
  • GoPro with waterproof cases, extra batteries and SD cards.
  • Money for the ferry tickets and island meals. US dollars work everywhere.
